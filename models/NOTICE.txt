Spleeter 4-stem models
======================

spleeter_{vocals,drums,bass,other}.onnx

Model architecture and weights: Deezer Spleeter
  https://github.com/deezer/spleeter
  MIT licence (per the Spleeter authors' JOSS paper, doi:10.21105/joss.02154)

ONNX export: Best-Practice/spleeter-4stems-onnx
  https://huggingface.co/Best-Practice/spleeter-4stems-onnx
  Apache-2.0

The export derives from sherpa-onnx's PyTorch port of Spleeter's U-Net
(Apache-2.0), with the activation functions parameterised so that the 4stems
configuration uses ELU instead of the 2stems LeakyReLU/ReLU defaults.

HT-Demucs v4 model (optional, high quality stems)
=================================================

htdemucs.onnx (~316 MB) is NOT bundled by default; it is downloaded on demand
by the user into the models folder.

Model architecture and weights: HT-Demucs (Hybrid Transformer Demucs), Meta AI
  https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs
  MIT licence
  Rouard, Massa, Defossez, "Hybrid Transformers for Music Source Separation",
  ICASSP 2023

ONNX export: StemSplitio/htdemucs-onnx
  https://huggingface.co/StemSplitio/htdemucs-onnx
  MIT licence

Model I/O: input "mix" float32 [1, 2, 343980] (7.8 s, 44.1 kHz stereo),
output "stems" float32 [1, 4, 2, 343980] in the order drums, bass, other,
vocals.

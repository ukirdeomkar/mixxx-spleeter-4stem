Spleeter 4-stem models
======================

spleeter_{vocals,drums,bass,other}.onnx

Model architecture and weights: Deezer Spleeter
  https://github.com/deezer/spleeter
  MIT licence (per the Spleeter authors' JOSS paper, doi:10.21105/joss.02154)

ONNX export: Best-Practice/spleeter-4stems-onnx
  https://huggingface.co/Best-Practice/spleeter-4stems-onnx
  Apache-2.0

The export derives from sherpa-onnx's PyTorch port of Spleeter's U-Net
(Apache-2.0), with the activation functions parameterised so that the 4stems
configuration uses ELU instead of the 2stems LeakyReLU/ReLU defaults.
